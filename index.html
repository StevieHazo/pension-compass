Open methodology

Calculation sources

Compensation calculator

The engine translates the formula layers from the April 2024 Voluntary Exit and VR/CR workbooks. It includes salary thresholds, tariff caps, pension-age tapering, part-time restrictions, qualifying service and statutory redundancy comparison.

Lump sum explorer

The alpha illustration uses £12 of lump sum for each £1 of annual pension exchanged. It does not calculate the maximum amount.

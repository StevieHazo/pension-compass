The rule used

For this alpha illustration, every £1 of annual pension given up produces £12 of lump sum.

Annual pension exchanged=Chosen lump sum ÷ 12

Important limits

  • The app does not calculate the maximum lump sum permitted.
  • It does not calculate tax or promise that a lump sum will be tax-free.
  • It does not calculate early or late retirement factors.
  • It does not connect to pension records or confirm entitlement.

Privacy first

Your figures stay on your device

The calculator works without an account and does not send the amounts you enter to a server.

Calculator data

The calculation takes place inside your browser. This build does not save or transmit your pension figures.

Advertising

No live advertising or analytics service is connected. Before either is enabled, the site will need production consent controls, a cookie register and an updated privacy notice.

Your choice

No non-essential technology choice has been stored.

Independent by design

Advertising will never shape your result

The core calculator is intended to remain free. Any future adverts will be clearly labelled and kept away from the calculator controls and result.

  • Advertisers do not change or approve calculations.
  • Adverts will not appear between input fields or beside action buttons.
  • Adverts will not appear inside the result card.
  • Calculator inputs and outputs will not be sent to advertisers.
  • The calculator will remain usable if non-essential technologies are rejected.
